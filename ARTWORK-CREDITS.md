# Third-walk reference

Vincent van Gogh, Café Terrace at Night, 1888. Kröller-Müller Museum.

Reference image: The Yorck Project reproduction, Wikimedia Commons. Public-domain artwork and reproduction, as identified on the source page:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_Willem_van_Gogh_-_Cafe_Terrace_at_Night_(Yorck).jpg

Downloaded 2026-09-24 and resized for the in-scene reference card.

The scanned Columbus street is the student's own scan. Its point-cloud conversion, navigation, and interactive color treatment were developed with AI coding assistance. The third walk is a color interpretation inspired by the painting, not a reconstruction of its architecture.
